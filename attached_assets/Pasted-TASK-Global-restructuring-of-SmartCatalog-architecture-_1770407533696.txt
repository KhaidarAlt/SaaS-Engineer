TASK: Global restructuring of SmartCatalog architecture

LANGUAGE: Russian  
GOAL: support 3 business niches with independent logic,
styles and WAU features.

────────────────────────────────
1. ARCHITECTURE CHANGE
────────────────────────────────

• Delete standalone section “Товары”
• Introduce new core section:

📌 “Шаблоны каталога”

Subsections:
- Universal
- Fashion
- Food

“Товары” become CONTEXT-DEPENDENT
and open inside selected template.

2. TEMPLATE SYSTEM
────────────────────────────────

Each template defines:

- product fields  
- catalog UX  
- checkout logic  
- AI behavior  
- WAU mechanics
