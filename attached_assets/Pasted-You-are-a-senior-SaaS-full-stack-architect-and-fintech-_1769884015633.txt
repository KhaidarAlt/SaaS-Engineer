Build a production-ready payment module for a SaaS platform called “SmartCatalog”.

SmartCatalog already has:
- WhatsApp integration with AI assistant
- Internal CRM (Orders)
- External CRM integrations (Bitrix24, amoCRM)
- Telegram notifications

Your task:
Implement a NEW CORE FEATURE — a dedicated “Платежи” section that enables
accepting payments via Kaspi directly from WhatsApp and automatically detecting payment status.

INTERFACE LANGUAGE: **RUSSIAN ONLY**

────────────────────────────────
CORE PRINCIPLES
────────────────────────────────
1. SmartCatalog is NOT a payment provider
2. Each business user connects their OWN Kaspi Business account
3. SmartCatalog orchestrates payments, does not store money
4. Payments can be marked as PAID automatically OR manually
5. Internal CRM is the source of truth
6. External CRM must stay in sync

────────────────────────────────
PAYMENT FLOW (AUTOMATIC)
────────────────────────────────
1. Client places order in WhatsApp
2. AI assistant calculates final amount
3. SmartCatalog creates Kaspi payment
4. AI sends payment link in the SAME WhatsApp chat
5. Client clicks link → redirected to Kaspi app
6. Client sees amount and pays
7. SmartCatalog receives webhook or checks status
8. On success:
   - payment_status = PAID
   - order_status = “Оплачен”
   - trigger notifications and CRM sync

────────────────────────────────
PAYMENT FLOW (MANUAL)
────────────────────────────────
- User manually sets order status to “Оплачен”
- payment_source = manual
- payment_status remains unchanged
- External CRM sync is triggered
